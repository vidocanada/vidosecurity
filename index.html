SWIR Image Technology

SenSWIR technology provide photodiodes on an InGaAs compound semiconductor layer and are connected via Cu-Cu connection with a silicon layer forming the readout circuit — a design which enables high-sensitivity over a broad range of wavelengths.

SWIR imaging with InGaAs-based cameras is well suited to nondestructive identification of materials, their composition, coatings, and other characteristics... Whatever your specific applications, our experts can help you optimize your imaging system to maximize imaging performance.
